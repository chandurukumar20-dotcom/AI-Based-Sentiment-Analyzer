Three-Class Sentiment Architecture

Unlike binary models that force neutral remarks into arbitrary positive or negative categories, our architecture employs calibrated NLP thresholds to distinguish between satisfied, dissatisfied, and factual statements:

Positive Sentiment
High customer satisfaction, praise, delight, and product appreciation.
Negative Sentiment
Complaints, dissatisfaction, defect reports, and customer frustration.
Neutral Sentiment
Factual statements, inquiries, shipping confirmations, and objective comments.
